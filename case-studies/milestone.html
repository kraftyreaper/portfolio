Client
Milestone
Overview
Milestone is an app that helps parents teach their kids about money. The goal is to make the next generation confident with finances. In a world where people want things quickly, it's important for parents to show their kids how to handle money wisely. The Milestone app has tools to teach kids about saving, allowances, spending, and more in a safe and easy way.

Client Vision

  • Milestone envisions being a comprehensive financial literacy app, aiming to empower parents in cultivating a financially confident generation.
  • To design the app in a way that addresses these concerns, ensuring that it promotes healthy usage without becoming addictive.
  • To design engaging features and content to capture and maintain the interest of young users.
  • To incorporate motivational elements and positive reinforcement to keep kids engaged in the learning process.
  • To ensure the app met the strict guidelines of app stores to gain approval and maintain credibility.
  • To design collaborative features that involve parents in the financial education process, addressing the challenge of parental engagement.
  • To carefully consider the design elements to encourage regular usage while avoiding negative impacts on children's behavior.
  • To break down financial lessons into manageable, bite-sized chunks (Quests) and use gamification to make learning enjoyable.
  • Improve the communication of Milestone's brand identity through the design and functionality of the mobile app.
  • Create intuitive and engaging user journeys within the app to encourage sustained interaction and learning for both parents and kids.
  • Design the app to be a collaborative platform where parents can guide their kids in learning essential money management skills, fostering financial literacy.
  • Encourage user engagement by designing workflows that captivate both parents and kids, making the app a valuable and enjoyable educational tool.
  • Incorporate features and elements that motivate kids through positive reinforcement, allowing them to achieve goals and develop good financial habits.
  • Validate app workflows and features with the target audience (parents and kids) through prototypes, ensuring that the proposed functionalities align with user expectations.
  • Collaborate with other vendors, such as those engaged with Milestone, to establish a cohesive visual identity for the product that aligns with brand values and resonates with the target audience.

Concept to Launch

1. Discovery and Planning

  • Identified challenges and concerns from Milestone founders.
  • Defined project objectives and requirements.
  • Explored key parameters for success, considering user behaviors of both children and parents.
  • Conceptualized the components of the app based on the identified user behaviors.
  • Formulated hypotheses for the key functionality of the app.

2. Product Documentation

  • Clearly outlined the vision and goals of Milestone – a financial literacy app for children.
  • Defined the target audience, focusing on parents and kids in the APAC region.
  • Feature Specification: Detailed the essential features, including guided onboarding, collaborative goal setting, positive reinforcement through chores, bite-sized learning quests, and gamified milestones. Documented the inclusion of an inclusive 'Guardians' concept, allowances, interest, and other key functionalities.
  • Requirements Analysis: Conducted thorough analysis to identify technical and user requirements. Documented functionalities necessary for seamless user experience and efficient app management.

3. Mid-fidelity Design

  • Wireframing: Developed mid-fidelity wireframes outlining the app's basic structure, layout, and functionality. Ensured wireframes reflected collaborative goal setting, positive reinforcement, and other essential features.
  • Prototyping: Constructed interactive prototypes using tools like Figma to simulate user journeys. Validated hypotheses and gathered feedback from stakeholders and potential users.

4. Design System

  • Visual Identity: Collaborated with vendors to establish the visual identity for Milestone. Incorporated branding elements into UI designs, ensuring alignment with the brand's values.
  • Design Components: Developed a set of design components and patterns for consistent use across the app. Ensured a cohesive design language for both the parent and child-facing mobile apps.

5. High-fidelity Design

  • Detailed UI Design: Enhanced wireframes into high-fidelity designs with detailed visual elements. Ensured the designs maintained a balance between aesthetics and usability.
  • User Feedback Iterations: Incorporated feedback from stakeholders and user testing into the high-fidelity design. Made necessary adjustments to enhance the overall user experience.

6. Development

  • Frontend and Backend Development: Collaborated with development teams to implement the designed features. Ensured alignment with the established design system and maintained design integrity.
  • CI/CD Implementation: Set up Continuous Integration/Continuous Deployment pipelines for efficient development and deployment processes. Monitored progress to ensure timely delivery of features.

7. QA/UAT

  • Testing Strategy: Developed a comprehensive testing strategy covering test cases and scenarios for each functionality.
  • User Acceptance Testing (UAT): Engaged stakeholders and potential users in UAT to validate the app's usability and functionality. Addressed identified issues promptly and iteratively improved the app.
