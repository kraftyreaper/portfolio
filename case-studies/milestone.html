1. Research and discovery

  • Researching gamification inside top tier educational tech apps and understanding behavioral hooks for engaging Gen Z learning.

2. Product documentation

Mapping out the "Quest" and "Reward" user journeys and compiling interaction requirements in a PRD to keep product managers and developers aligned on core deliverables.

3. Mid-fidelity design

Wireframing the core gamified loops, ensuring the interface is intuitive and friction-free for children while keeping high-level controls easily accessible for parents.

4. Design system

Establishing an energetic, playful, and responsive design system tailored to a younger demographic. Created reusable atomic components to unify app interfaces.

5. High-fidelity design

Designing vibrant, visually appealing UI screens with playful colors, custom iconography, and micro-animations to consistently reward progress and drive retention.

6. Development

Working closely with frontend and backend developers to implement complex gamification logic, interactive gestures, and ensure a seamless mobile application experience.

7. QA and testing

  • Thorough testing of game mechanics, reward triggers, and parental verification controls.
  • Validating mobile accessibility, high-contrast text, and responsive layout across iOS and Android devices.
