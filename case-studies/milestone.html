Overview
Milestone is an app that helps parents teach their kids about money. The goal is to make the next generation confident with finances. In a world where people want things quickly, it's important for parents to show their kids how to handle money wisely. The Milestone app has tools to teach kids about saving, allowances, spending, and more in a safe and easy way.

Behavioral Design for Families

Milestone is a financial literacy platform designed for the APAC region. My challenge was to bridge the gap between **Parental Governance** and **Child Engagement**, ensuring the app was educational without being addictive or violating minor privacy (COPPA-aligned thinking).

Gamification: Rewards without addiction loops
  • The Drop-off Point: Parents often start financial apps but lose momentum. I designed "Guardians" features to distribute the educational load across the family.
  • Attention vs. Learning: Balancing flashy "Quests" with actual retention of financial concepts like 'Interest' and 'Spending'.
  • Minor Security: Designing 'Safe-Space' UI where kids can learn without direct exposure to bank API complexities.
  • Governance: Implemented a "Parental Override" system for all chore/reward transactions to maintain real-world discipline.

Instead of generic badges, I implemented Value-Driven Gamification. Kids earn "Purchasing Power" tied to real-world allowance, turning digital progress into tangible financial discipline. This creates a feedback loop that parents value and children enjoy.

Concept to Launch

1. Discovery & Planning

  • Mapped the separate goals of Parents (Teaching resilience) and Kids (Immediate rewards) to form a dual-persona strategy.
  • Conducted focus groups to identify friction points in typical chore-allowance cycles.
  • Outcome: Hypothesized the 'Guardian' concept to balance financial learning with parental control, preventing app abandonment.

2. Product Documentation

  • Detailed the core features: guided onboarding, collaborative goal setting, and positive reinforcement loops.
  • Designed the 'Permission Handshake'—a unique UX pattern for parental approval of kid-initiated tasks.
  • Outcome: Structured a PRD that aligned product managers and developers on complex family-account edge cases.

3. Mid-fidelity Design

  • Developed wireframes outlining the app's basic structure and core financial loops.
  • Prototyped "Bite-sized Quests" to ensure the app encourages learning rather than screen-time addiction.
  • Outcome: Validated the 'Allowance' and 'Quest' visual models with stakeholders, establishing an intuitive foundation.

4. Design System

  • Created a scalable design system with two distinct brand voices: Clean/Governed for parents, Playful/Tactile for kids.
  • Developed custom iconography to translate complex financial concepts (savings, interest, spending) for children.
  • Outcome: Established reusable atomic components that ensure consistent UI implementation across both mobile environments.

5. High-fidelity Design

  • Enhanced wireframes into vibrant, visually appealing screens that balance playful aesthetics with strict usability.
  • Applied micro-interactions and tactile haptic feedback to reward kids for hitting 'Saving Goals'.
  • Outcome: Delivered a polished, highly responsive interface that drives engagement and received unanimous positive user feedback.

6. Development

  • Worked closely with engineering to implement complex gamification logic and dual-app API handshakes.
  • Ensured strict implementation of the design system components to maintain absolute visual integrity.
  • Outcome: Overcame technical constraints related to animated interactions without compromising the premium feel of the product.

7. QA & Testing

  • Tested the 'Reward triggers' to ensure notifications aren't spammy or fatigue-inducing for busy parents.
  • Collaborated on security and usability audits regarding the handling of minor user profiles.
  • Outcome: Achieved a successful, stable app launch with zero critical usability hurdles, ensuring a safe digital environment for kids.
