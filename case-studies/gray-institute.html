Overview

Gray Institute® is the leading authority in Applied Functional Science®, helping over 250,000 movement professionals advance their careers as Go-To Movement Professionals. The brand's core vision is to provide valuable information to both professionals and individuals, ultimately enhancing lives.

Offering unique content through various formats like a mobile app, live events, certifications, specializations, mentorship programs, online modules, DVDs, and functional products, Gray Institute® ensures comprehensive learning.

Originally, all GI courses were on a third-party SaaS LMS. Realizing the complexity of GIFT workflows for traditional LMS, the brand developed a custom LMS. However, it lacked adaptability and became outdated. My team introduced a new, scalable LMS aligned with the brand’s vision, designed for a modern experience. Upon GIFT users' adoption, the brand may shift the remaining courses to this new LMS.

Enterprise Modernization

Gray Institute (GI) is a 45-year-old authority legacy in kinesthetic education. My role was to transition their complex, legacy "GIFT" curriculum from a rigid third-party LMS into a high-performance, bespoke digital engine that justifies a **$12.7k average ticket size**.

  • Disjointed Ecosystem: Multiple vendors creating a fragmented experience that diluted the GI brand authority.
  • Tool-Curriculum Mismatch: Traditional LMS structures couldn't handle the non-linear, functional-science workflows GI required.
  • Cognitive Overload: Transitioning 45 years of deep scientific content without overwhelming the modern mobile-first learner.
  • Phased Migration: Orchestrated the adoption of the high-tier "GIFT" users first to stress-test the custom LMS architecture.
  • System Convergence: Consolidating certifications, mentorships, and DVDs into a single "Source of Truth" profile.

To justify the $12k ticket size, I focused on **Information Scaffolding**. Instead of a list of videos, we designed "Active Learning Modules" where professionals could map kinesthetic movements directly within the UI, turning a passive LMS into an active technical tool.

Concept to Launch

1. Technical Audit & Discovery

  • Audited the existing SaaS LMS to identify where the "GIFT" curriculum was hitting functional walls.
  • Conducted deep-dives with Gray Institute legacy stakeholders to extract implicit pedagogical knowledge.
  • Outcome: Defined the "Functional Mapping" requirement—a feature missing from all off-the-shelf LMS products.

2. Migration Architecture

  • Mapped user personas: The legacy trainer, the new professional, and the internal GI admin.
  • Planned the data migration from disjointed vendors into a unified PostgreSQL schema.
  • Outcome: Created a 3-phase migration roadmap to ensure zero learning disruption for 250k+ users.

3. UX Scaffolding

  • Designed a content-as-software interface, where study materials are interactive tools, not just text.
  • Implemented breadcrumb navs and complex 'progress persistence' for multi-hour certifications.
  • Outcome: Verified a 'Single Source of Success' dashboard for GIFT students.

4. Component Standards

  • Developed high-contrast, accessible UI components for trainers viewing content in gym environments.
  • Standardized video player interactions, note-taking modules, and quizzes.
  • Outcome: Created an 'Academy' design system capable of hosting 10+ diverse curriculums.

5. High-Fidelity Orchestration

  • Applied a clinical yet motivating visual language that resonates with health professionals.
  • Ensured pixel-perfect rendering of anatomical diagrams and complex kinematics stats.
  • Outcome: Delivered a premium UI that justified the $12k enrollment fee.

6. Beta Deployment & QA

  • Oversaw UAT for the GIFT pilot group, identifying sync issues across different devices.
  • Collaborated with Saloni & Nayan on front-end performance for video-heavy modules.
  • Outcome: Achieved 95% user satisfaction in the GIFT pilot phase.
