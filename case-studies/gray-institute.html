Overview

Gray Institute® is the leading authority in Applied Functional Science®, helping over 250,000 movement professionals advance their careers as Go-To Movement Professionals. The brand's core vision is to provide valuable information to both professionals and individuals, ultimately enhancing lives.

Offering unique content through various formats like a mobile app, live events, certifications, specializations, mentorship programs, online modules, DVDs, and functional products, Gray Institute® ensures comprehensive learning.

Originally, all GI courses were on a third-party SaaS LMS. Realizing the complexity of GIFT workflows for traditional LMS, the brand developed a custom LMS. However, it lacked adaptability and became outdated. My team introduced a new, scalable LMS aligned with the brand’s vision, designed for a modern experience. Upon GIFT users' adoption, the brand may shift the remaining courses to this new LMS.

Enterprise Modernization

Gray Institute (GI) is a 45-year-old authority legacy in kinesthetic education. My role was to transition their complex, legacy "GIFT" curriculum from a rigid third-party LMS into a high-performance, bespoke digital engine that justifies a **$12.7k average ticket size**.

  • Disjointed Ecosystem: Multiple vendors creating a fragmented experience that diluted the GI brand authority.
  • Tool-Curriculum Mismatch: Traditional LMS structures couldn't handle the non-linear, functional-science workflows GI required.
  • Cognitive Overload: Transitioning 45 years of deep scientific content without overwhelming the modern mobile-first learner.
  • Phased Migration: Orchestrated the adoption of the high-tier "GIFT" users first to stress-test the custom LMS architecture.
  • System Convergence: Consolidating certifications, mentorships, and DVDs into a single "Source of Truth" profile.

To justify the $12k ticket size, I focused on **Information Scaffolding**. Instead of a list of videos, we designed "Active Learning Modules" where professionals could map kinesthetic movements directly within the UI, turning a passive LMS into an active technical tool.

Concept to Launch

1. Research & Discovery

  • Audited the legacy SaaS LMS to identify where the "GIFT" curriculum was hitting functional and pedagogical walls.
  • Conducted deep-dive interviews with Gray Institute stakeholders to extract and codify implicit biomechanical knowledge.
  • Outcome: Defined the foundational "Functional Mapping" requirement—a complex feature missing from off-the-shelf LMS products.

2. Product Documentation

  • Mapped distinct user personas: the legacy trainer, the new professional student, and the internal GI administrator.
  • Architected the data migration blueprint, consolidating disjointed vendor schemas into a unified system structure.
  • Outcome: Created a 3-phase strategic migration roadmap ensuring zero learning disruption for over 250k active users.

3. Mid-Fidelity Design

  • Designed a "content-as-software" interface, transforming static study materials into interactive, kinesthetic tools.
  • Implemented rigorous UX scaffolding, including complex progress persistence for multi-hour certifications.
  • Outcome: Verified a 'Single Source of Truth' modular dashboard layout before applying visual weight.

4. Design System

  • Developed high-contrast, accessible UI components tailored for trainers viewing content in bright gym environments.
  • Standardized complex interaction patterns across video players, granular note-taking modules, and quizzes.
  • Outcome: Established a scalable 'Academy' design ecosystem capable of hosting 10+ diverse biomechanics curriculums.

5. High-Fidelity Design

  • Applied a clinical yet motivating visual language designed to resonate with elite health and fitness professionals.
  • Ensured pixel-perfect rendering of anatomical diagrams, complex kinematics stats, and immersive video playback.
  • Outcome: Delivered a premium UI experience that visually justified and elevated the $12k enrollment fee.

6. Development Handoff & Support

  • Collaborated directly with engineering teams to ensure accurate implementation of interactive video modules.
  • Provided clear interaction specs and state management logic for the complex biomechanical analysis tools.
  • Outcome: Bridged the gap between clinical design intent and functional deployment without compromising performance.

7. QA & Testing

  • Oversaw rigorous User Acceptance Testing (UAT) for the primary GIFT pilot group.
  • Identified and resolved crucial synchronization edge cases and front-end performance bottlenecks for video-heavy modules.
  • Outcome: Received high acclaim from the pilot cohort and successfully validated the new LMS architecture for global rollout.
