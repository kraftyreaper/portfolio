Overview

ASVI Thoughtworks offers integrated HR solutions for large, small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs) in different industries. Their services include Virtual HR, COE Advisory, and Resource Management, aiming to enhance scalability and streamline HR functions for clients.

The team at ASVI Thoughtworks acknowledges the vital role of a robust and enduring workforce in the prosperity of any business. Comprising skilled HR professionals, they are dedicated to providing tailored services to assist clients in attracting, retaining, and nurturing top talent.

Service Design & Digital Maturity

ASVI Thoughtworks is a fractional HR consultancy transitioning from an offline, boutique service into a scalable digital platform. My challenge was not just building a website, but **architecting the service delivery model** that could handle diverse SME industries without manual overhead.

Offline
Transition: Moving boutique consultancy to SaaS-lite
SME
Scalability: Tailored HR flows for varied verticals
Systemic
Org Mapping: Digitalizing complex reporting lines
  • Implicit knowledge: Extracting HR logic from senior consultants' heads and turning it into digital triggers.
  • The Trust Gap: Moving long-term clients from 'calling their consultant' to 'logging into a dashboard' without losing the personal touch.
  • Legacy to Logic: Standardizing disparate HR practices (Virtual HR, COE Advisory) into a unified UI framework.
  • Brand Credibility: Establishing a digital-first identity that commands as much respect as their 50+ years of collective experience.

I approached this as a Service Design Problem first. We didn't just design buttons; we designed a 'Service Layer' that allows ASVI consultants to manage multiple client organizational trees, skill assessments, and learning programs from a single governed cockpit.

Concept to Launch

1. Service Discovery & Logic Extraction

  • Conducted deep-dive workshops with senior HR consultants to extract their 'Implicit Logic' for talent management.
  • Audited the offline 'Spreadsheet culture' to identify data dependencies for the digital transition.
  • Outcome: Defined the CORE-7 workflows that would survive the digital migration.

2. Systemic Org Architecture

  • Designed a flexible 'Org-Tree' engine capable of hosting diverse SME structures (flat vs. hierarchical).
  • Mapped the service blueprint: User Trigger -> Consultant Action -> System Notification.
  • Outcome: Created a unified PRD for a combined branding site and complex HR dashboard.

3. Digital Brand Identity

  • Translated the boutique, 'high-touch' consulting values into a modern digital visual language.
  • Developed a typography and color system that communicates 'Thought Leadership' and 'Strategic Precision'.
  • Outcome: Delivered a cohesive brand identity that positioned them as a tech-forward consultancy.

4. Strategic Webflow Orchestration

  • Built the external brand presence on Webflow, ensuring high performance and SEO readiness.
  • Implemented responsive layouts that maintain a 'Premium Editorial' feel across all viewports.
  • Outcome: Increased digital inbound leads by establishing immediate brand authority.

5. Complex Dashboard Logic

  • Prototyped the 'Consultant Cockpit'—a dashboard for HR leads to manage multiple client accounts simultaneously.
  • User-tested the Skill Assessment flow to ensure low cognitive load for SME employees.
  • Outcome: Verified a 'Single Source of Success' for organizational monitoring.

6. Full-Fidelity Governance

  • Applied full-fidelity UI to the dashboard, ensuring consistency with the Webflow brand site.
  • Orchestrated the handoff to developer teams with clear logic for 'Org-Tree' state management.
  • Outcome: Successful transition of the first 3 pilot clients onto the digital platform.
