Concept to Launch

1. Branding - Onboarding questionnaires

  • Developed detailed onboarding questionnaires to delve into the nuances of ASVI Thoughtworks. Explored aspects such as business objectives, website requirements, tone of voice, brand personality, core brand fundamentals, target audience, and a thorough understanding of competitors and third-party tools.

2. Branding - Service design architecture

  • Conceptualized and designed a service design architecture that outlined the workflow and services provided by ASVI Thoughtworks and its clients.
  • Problems: Mapped out tools, frameworks, and processes to address identified problems.
  • Deliverables: Specified tangible outcomes and outputs expected from the project.
  • Impact of Problem-Solving: Explored the potential impact of addressing identified issues.
  • Offerings and Services: Defined the range of services ASVI Thoughtworks would provide through the platform.

3. Branding - Secondary research

  • Conducted thorough secondary research to analyze competitors in the HR solutions space. Evaluated their strengths and weaknesses, allowing us to refine our own strategy and design based on industry best practices. Integrated findings into the service design architecture to ensure differentiation and innovation.

4. Branding - Brand visual identity design

  • Development of Cohesive Visual Identity:
  • Translated the brand personality and fundamentals into a cohesive visual identity. Created a distinctive logo, color palette, typography, and other design elements that represented ASVI Thoughtworks' values and objectives.

5. Branding - Website design on webflow

  • Execution of Web Design:
  • Translated the brand visual identity into the design of the ASVI Thoughtworks website using Webflow. Ensured a user-friendly and visually appealing interface that reflected the brand's professionalism and commitment to innovation.

6. Product Design - Goal definition of stakeholder

  • Utilizing Service Design Architecture
  • Leveraged the previously defined service design architecture to set clear and achievable goals for each stakeholder involved in the HR platform. Documented these goals to serve as a guide for the subsequent design phases.

7. Product Design - Mid-fidelity design

  • Development of Mid-Fidelity Wireframes and User Flows: Created mid-fidelity wireframes and mapped out the user journey for each stakeholder. Developed wireframes and user flows to visually represent the interaction and navigation within the platform.
  • Stakeholder Testing and Feedback: Conducted testing with relevant stakeholders to gather valuable feedback on the mid-fidelity design. Incorporated suggestions and refinements to ensure the final product aligns with user expectations and needs.

8. Product Design - High-fidelity design

  • Cohesive Brand Integration:
  • Infused brand identity elements into the mid-fidelity design to maintain consistency with the established visual identity. Ensured that the user experience aligns seamlessly with the overall brand image. Awaiting client approval before moving on to the development phase.
