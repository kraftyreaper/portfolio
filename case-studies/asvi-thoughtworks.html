Overview

ASVI Thoughtworks offers integrated HR solutions for large, small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs) in different industries. Their services include Virtual HR, COE Advisory, and Resource Management, aiming to enhance scalability and streamline HR functions for clients.

The team at ASVI Thoughtworks acknowledges the vital role of a robust and enduring workforce in the prosperity of any business. Comprising skilled HR professionals, they are dedicated to providing tailored services to assist clients in attracting, retaining, and nurturing top talent.

Client Vision

  • The organization envisions empowering companies and their workers by overseeing the complete lifecycle management. The focus is on promoting career advancement and establishing positive work environments, ensuring that each person makes a meaningful contribution to the success of the business.
  • Before the project, ASVI Thoughtworks operated primarily offline, lacking a digital presence. The challenge was to transition the organization into a digital landscape, requiring a strategic and comprehensive approach.
  • The client, seeking a robust digital solution, had high expectations for the platform's functionality and user experience. Meeting these expectations while aligning with the brand's values was a continuous challenge.
  • ASVI Thoughtworks offered a range of HR solutions, including Virtual HR, COE Advisory, and Resource Management. Integrating these diverse functions into a unified platform posed a challenge, requiring a cohesive service design architecture.
  • The primary objective was to digitize ASVI Thoughtworks' HR operations, enabling the organization to manage tasks, create and manage the organizational tree, assess employee skills, assign learning programs, and facilitate promotions or demotions.
  • Beyond functional objectives, establishing a strong digital brand presence was crucial. This included developing a cohesive visual identity, a professional website, and integrating brand elements into the digital platform.
  • The platform aimed to streamline HR functions for SMEs, providing an integrated solution for organizational task management and employee development. This involved creating a seamless user experience for various stakeholders within the organization.

Concept to Launch

1. Understanding Business - Onboard Questionnaries

  • Conducted detailed onboarding questionnaires to delve into the nuances of ASVI Thoughtworks. Explored aspects such as business objectives, website requirements, tone of voice, brand personality, core brand fundamentals, target audience, and a thorough understanding of competitors and third-party tools.

2. Service Design Architecture

Outlined a comprehensive service design architecture encompassing:

  • Problem Statements: Identified key challenges faced by ASVI Thoughtworks and its clients.
  • Solutions: Mapped out tools, frameworks, and processes to address identified problems.
  • Deliverables: Specified tangible outcomes and outputs expected from the project.
  • Impact of Problem-Solving: Explored the potential impact of addressing identified issues.
  • Offerings and Services: Defined the range of services ASVI Thoughtworks would provide through the platform.

3. Secondary Research

  • Conducted thorough secondary research to analyze competitors in the HR solutions space. Evaluated their strengths and weaknesses, allowing us to refine our own strategy and design based on industry best practices. Integrated findings into the service design architecture to ensure differentiation and innovation.

4. Brand Visual Identity Design

  • Translated the brand personality and fundamentals into a cohesive visual identity. Created a distinctive logo, color palette, typography, and other design elements that represented ASVI Thoughtworks' values and objectives.

5. Website Design on Webflow

  • Implemented the brand visual identity into the design of the ASVI Thoughtworks website using Webflow. Ensured a user-friendly and visually appealing interface that reflected the brand's professionalism and commitment to innovation.

6. Goal Definition for Stakeholders

  • Leveraged the previously defined service design architecture to set clear and achievable goals for each stakeholder involved in the HR platform. Documented these goals to serve as a guide for the subsequent design phases.

7. Mid-fidelity Design

  • User Journey Definition: Commenced mid-fidelity design to outline the user journey for each stakeholder. Developed wireframes and user flows to visually represent the interaction and navigation within the platform.
  • Stakeholder Testing and Feedback: Conducted testing with relevant stakeholders to gather valuable feedback on the mid-fidelity design. Incorporated suggestions and refinements to ensure the final product aligns with user expectations and needs.

8. Brand Integration in Mid-Fidelity

  • Infused brand identity elements into the mid-fidelity design to maintain consistency with the established visual identity. Ensured that the user experience aligns seamlessly with the overall brand image. Awaiting client approval before moving on to the development phase.

9. High-Fidelity Design and Client Approval

  • Advanced to the high-fidelity design phase to create a polished and refined user interface. Focused on visual details, interactions, and a seamless user experience. Awaiting client approval before moving on to the development phase.
