Human Resources (HR) and Business Services

Asvi Thoughtworks - Strategic Fractional HR Services for business.

asvithoughtworks.com

Challenges

Asvi Thoughtworks provides strategic Fractional HR services for high-growth businesses. The primary challenge was communicating a complex, intangible service to founders who need immediate results. Key hurdles included:

  • Conceptual Ambiguity: "Fractional HR" is a new concept for many Indian founders. We had to define the "What" and "How" clearly.
  • Value Articulation: Positioning Asvi as a strategic partner rather than just a recruitment vendor.
  • Brand Scalability: Building a visual identity that felt professional, established, and tech-forward.
  • Information Architecture: Organizing a wide range of HR services into a digestible service design architecture.

Objectives

  • Establish Trust: Design a digital presence that reflects Asvi's deep HR expertise and strategic mindset.
  • Clear Conversion Path: Create a journey from understanding the fractional model to booking a stakeholder interview.
  • Scalable Design: Develop a branding and UI system that could evolve as Asvi adds new service modules.
Research

Product Documentation

Conducted detailed onboarding questionnaires to delve into the nuances of ASVI Thoughtworks. Explored aspects such as business objectives, website requirements, tone of voice, brand personality, core brand fundamentals, target audience, and a thorough understanding of competitors and third-party tools.

Branding

Service Design Architecture

Definition of Service Design Architecture

Outlined a comprehensive service design architecture encompassing:

  • Modular Onboarding: A structured intake process to identify client-specific HR needs.
  • Resource Allocation: Dynamic mapping of HR specialists to high-impact project areas.
  • Feedback Loops: Built-in mechanisms for continuous improvement between Asvi and its clients.
