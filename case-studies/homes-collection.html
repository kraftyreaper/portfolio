High fragmentation in the luxury real estate market and the need for a seamless, trustworthy digital-first buying experience for high-value transactions.

Establishing a premium brand identity that commands trust, streamlining the property discovery process, and creating a high-conversion lead generation funnel for NRI and HNI segments.

Concept to Launch

1. Research and discovery

  • Research about the real estate industry to better understand the concept of fractional ownership.
  • Multiple stakeholder interviews with the Homes Collection team to refine our understanding of their core requirements.
  • Competitor analysis to understand differentiations, USPs, processes that gave a view of the positioning of Homes Collection in the industry.

2. Product documentation

  • Compiling the research into data points to plan different user journeys for users to achieve their goal.
  • Documenting all journeys in a PRD (Product requirement documentation) that helps us keep everyone on the same track about the product.

3. Mid-fidelity design

  • Structuring all the documented data into web pages, ensuring accessibility through placements of the information at right place which helps the user achieve their goals at different touch points

4. Design system

  • Created the foundation of the design language to scale across web designs with uniformity and consistency. Created the design elements and converting them into components to facilitate effective work processes

5. High-fidelity design

  • Created high-fidelity design screens to enhance communication visually and elevate the brand's understanding using retentive brand elements. We also implemented a unique visual direction to bring in the premium look and feel of the business to create an aspirational value for the potential users of the properties.

6. Development

The challenges of building a user facing product and an admin dashboard for Homes Collection was to:

  • Integrate third party event management tool in our system, to keep a track of entire buying process from initiating user interest till getting the share transfer certificate
  • Integrating the third party tool to experience a property virtually
  • Our team analysed the above business requirements and the importance of the above features to enhance the users’ experience on the Homes collection platform. We converted the business requirements into the a user facing product and also created an admin dashboard for the client to seamlessly manage users’ data to help the business development needs.
  • The product requirement document is of major importance at this stage in bringing the client requirements and the development feasibilities in check.

7. QA and testing

  • Conduct end-to-end system testing to validate the overall functionality of the Homes Collection platform.
  • Test the admin dashboard to ensure seamless management of user data
  • Verify each functional component individually according to the high-fidelity design and PRD.
  • Test the different touchpoints to ensure users can achieve their goals effectively.
  • Validate the implementation of the design system to ensure uniformity and consistency.
  • Test the responsiveness and loading times of web pages to guarantee a smooth user experience.
