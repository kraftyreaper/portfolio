Challenges

Gray Institute is a world-renowned authority in Applied Functional Science. Their digital challenge was translating deep scientific knowledge into a modern learning platform. Key challenges included:

  • Content Density: Scientific educational material is dense and hard to navigate in a digital format.
  • Course Discovery: Professional physical therapists and trainers needed a faster way to find relevant specializations.
  • Brand Legacy: Balancing their established authority with a fresh, modern digital aesthetic.
  • User Journey: Fragmented learning paths across different physical therapy modules.

Objectives

  • Streamline Enrollment: Reduce the friction from landing on the homepage to starting the first course module.
  • Content Hierarchy: Organize Functional Science curriculum into a logical, progressive learning path.
  • Visual Authority: Elevate the brand through premium photography and a clean, healthcare-focused UI.
Research

Product Documentation

  • Document insights from stakeholder interviews regarding user types and their roles in course delivery.
